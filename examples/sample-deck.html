Invoice Intake Automation

OCR and RPA for purchase invoices

Welcome everyone. Keep this slide to 30 seconds.

Executive summary

  • Analysts key about 4,000 invoices a month by hand
  • OCR plus an RPA bot creates records and routes exceptions
  • Expected automation success rate is 60-70% initially

Assumptions

  1. Test data and lower-environment access are provided before build starts.
  2. Only standard purchase invoices are in scope for phase one.
  3. Exceptions are handled by analysts in a manual queue.

Scope

In scope

  • Standard purchase invoices from the shared mailbox
  • Field extraction and SOP validation

Out of scope

  • Handwritten invoices
  • Payment processing
